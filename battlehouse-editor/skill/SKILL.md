# BattleHouse Live clip style guide

> **Status: STARTER.** These rules are written from generic live-competition / creator-house
> clip conventions, *not* yet from real BattleHouse episodes (Season 1 premieres Oct 4, 2026).
> After the premiere, break down 10–20 official clips (see "Updating this guide" below) and

## The show in one paragraph
BattleHouse Live is a live-streamed reality competition: 30 creators in one mansion, $100,000 on
the line, hosted by Brandon Gomes and Coach K. Viewers vote and control what happens. It streams
live on YouTube, TikTok and Kick. Clips are cut from long stream VODs into vertical 9:16 shorts.

## What makes a clip-worthy moment (ranked)
1. **Vote results / eliminations** – the reveal itself plus the eliminated creator's reaction.
2. **Consequences landing** – the fan-voted punishment or twist hitting someone in real time.
3. **Challenge finishes** – the decisive moment of a challenge, close finishes, upsets.
4. **Conflict** – call-outs, beef, alliance betrayals, someone storming off.
5. **Host moments** – Brandon Gomes / Coach K reactions, announcements, roasts.
6. **Pure comedy** – a fail, a one-liner, chaos that needs no context.

Avoid: dead air, logistics, rules explanations, sponsor reads, anything that needs more than one
sentence of context to understand.

## Clip structure
- **Length:** 20–75 s. Default ~45 s. `[VERIFY]`
- **Start late:** begin 1–3 s before the payoff's setup, never at the start of a conversation.
  If the payoff is at the very end, open with a 1–2 s flash of it (cold open) — note it as a
  marker so the editor can copy it to the front.
- **End on the reaction**, not on the explanation afterwards. Cut within 1 s of the peak.
- **One story per clip.** If two things happen, make two clips.

## Hook (on-screen text, first 0–3 s)
- 3–8 words, ALL CAPS `[VERIFY]`, may include one emoji.
- States the stakes or tension, never the outcome: "CHAT VOTED HER OUT 😳" > "Jess gets eliminated".
- Use creator names/handles only if they're already known to the audience.

## Captions
- Word-by-word or 2–4 word chunks, centered lower-third. `[VERIFY]`
- Highlight the key word of each line (name, number, insult, "$100K"). `[VERIFY]`
- Censor slurs/profanity per platform.

## Sound & edit grammar  `[VERIFY all]`
- Whoosh/hit on the hook text, record-scratch or bass drop on reveals.
- Punch-in (110–130% zoom) on reactions and key lines.
- Cut dead air > 0.5 s between lines (jump cuts are fine on stream footage).
- Music ducked under dialogue; drop music to silence 0.5 s before a reveal.

## Post copy
- **Title** (YouTube Shorts): ≤ 60 chars, curiosity + stakes.
- **Caption** (TikTok/IG): 1–2 short lines + call to vote/watch live.
- **Hashtags:** #BattleHouseLive plus 2–4 relevant ones. `[VERIFY]`

## Notes for the editor (markers)
For each clip, leave timestamped notes the editor will see as Resolve markers, e.g.
"punch-in on reaction", "SFX hit", "bleep", "cold-open this line", "music drop".
