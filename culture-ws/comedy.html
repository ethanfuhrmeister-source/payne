What it
actually is

A weekly show for untested material. Comics bring the bit they wrote on Tuesday and find out on Thursday whether it works. Some of it will not. That is the point of the room.

It runs on the ninth floor of Liberty Plaza, in a space that is a gallery the rest of the week. Five dollars at the door, give or take. It starts at nine.
