What nail art costs

Art is priced as an add-on in five tiers, based on time, detail and materials — from $5 for a simple touch up to $75 for full custom work. We talk it through before I start and I quote you first, so nothing is a surprise.

  1. Tier One

    Simple Touch

    $5–$10

    Chrome finish, one color per nail, or minimal accents — lines, dots, or foil on a few fingers.

  2. Tier Two

    Mini Designs

    $10–$20

    Accent nail florals, small decals, stickers, French tips, or color blocking on a few fingers.

  3. Tier Three

    Mixed Art Set

    $20–$35

    A mix of designs across most nails — French with art, layered effects, swirl sets, abstract art.

  4. Tier Four

    Detailed Design

    $35–$50

    Hand-painted themes, multiple techniques in one set, detailed freehand or layered looks.

  5. Tier Five

    Full Custom Art

    $50–$75

    Character art (anime and cartoons), gem placements, sculpted 3D, isolated chrome per nail, full-set themed art. These take the most time and are one-of-a-kind.

Booking art? Send inspiration pictures when you book so I can work out the tier and prep for it. These tiers are the art add-on only — base prices for full sets, fills and manicures still to confirm.

Questions people ask

How do I book an appointment?

Send the form at the bottom of this page with what you'd like and a few days that work. I'll come back with times and confirm.

Does the welding hurt?

No. The weld happens away from the skin and takes a fraction of a second. Most people are surprised it's already done.

Can permanent jewelry come off?

Yes. You can open it with small scissors any time, and I can re-weld it later. It stays on through showers, swimming and sleeping.
