Questions people ask

How do I book an appointment?

Send the form at the bottom of this page with what you'd like and a few days that work. I'll come back with times and confirm.

Does the welding hurt?

No. The weld happens away from the skin and takes a fraction of a second. Most people are surprised it's already done.

Can permanent jewelry come off?

Yes. You can open it with small scissors any time, and I can re-weld it later. It stays on through showers, swimming and sleeping.
