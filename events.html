What actually happens at your event

I set up a small table wherever works — a corner of the reception, a back room, a booth. Guests come over when they want to, choose from chains laid out on a tray, and pick a length.

I size it to the wrist, join the two ends with a quick spark, and that's it. No clasp, nothing to fasten or lose. Most people are back with their drink inside two minutes.

Why people book it

It gives guests something to do that isn't standing around, and they leave wearing it. Bridesmaids end up with a matching bracelet none of them had to remember to buy. Birthday guests get a favor that doesn't get left on the table.

It also photographs well, which matters more than it sounds.

Questions people ask

Does it hurt?

No. The weld happens away from the skin and takes a fraction of a second. Most people are surprised it's already done.

How long does each guest take?

About a minute or two once they've picked a chain. For planning, figure roughly how many guests an hour.

Can it come off?

Yes. You can open it with a small pair of scissors any time, and I can re-weld it later. It stays on through showers, swimming and sleeping.

What do you need from us on the day?

A small table and two chairs, and somewhere near an outlet if possible. I bring everything else.

How far do you travel?

I cover your area and radius. If you're further out, ask anyway and I'll let you know.

How far ahead should we book?

Weekends in busy season go first. Send your date as early as you have it and I'll hold it while we sort the details.
